# Code of Conduct

We want a place where people can ask, correct and disagree about science and software without fear.

## Expected

- Be respectful, and assume good faith until shown otherwise.
- Criticize the work and the claim, not the person.
- Cite sources when you correct a fact.
- Accept that maintainers decide what this repository holds.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising and unrelated promotion.
- Posting credentials, personal data or material you have no right to share.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove a contribution, and may block a person who breaks these rules. To
report a problem, write to contact@alchemia.solutions with the link to what happened. Reports are handled
privately.
